The Lethecary: a first-person psychological horror game in Unreal Engine 5. You're a psychiatrist for people who survived something paranormal, and you're not human. You hypnotize your patients, step into their dreams, and rewrite the memory until they forget what they saw. A secret organization pays you to keep the world asleep. But every edit leaves a trace, and something in the dream has started following those traces back to you.

Being built in public. Follow the work in the [Journal](/journal/).
