Deadlight Tavern is the released game from Minnsace Inc. Steam lists the developer as minnsace, the release date as July 18, 2022, and the price as free to play. The store page describes it this way:

> Pour drinks, keep your regulars happy, and rake in tips. Then board up the windows and hold the bar when the horde arrives. Deadlight Tavern is a survival bar game for 1–4 players, set at the last cold beer before the end of the world.

The same listing calls it Action and Indie. A night is split between service and a horde: pour drinks and keep regulars happy, then defend the bar when the horde arrives. It supports single-player and online co-op for up to four players. The interface and subtitles are in English. A Steam account is required to download and play.

For the current build, system requirements, and download, use the Steam page.
